# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Snow stays visible on light backgrounds: flakes and piles get a cool outline. A new "Snow on" setting
  (Settings → Snow) follows the system appearance by default and can be forced to light or dark backgrounds.
- Spider-Man shoots a volley of webs at the piles ahead and each one clears the whole webbed area.
- Thor works across every snowy surface until all the stacked snow is gone.
- The snowman is built in stages (base, middle, head, then eyes, nose, smile, buttons, scarf, hat and arms).
- The snowball fight has two kids on opposite sides throwing at each other and across the screen at varied angles.

## 0.3.0

- Add four helpers: sliding penguin, snowball fight, snowman builder and Santa's sleigh flyby.
- Snow on windows follows the windows' rounded top corners.
- New flake shapes: a bold six-arm flake and one drawn from a bundled SVG, shown in equal numbers.
- Lower memory and power use: pause under full-screen apps, 30 FPS on battery, 15 FPS when away, no more idle-sleep
  blocking, and less memory when hidden.
- Add the app icon.

## 0.2.0

- Add Spider-Man, Thor and Iron Man helpers, selectable in the menu and in Settings.

## 0.1.0

- Menu-bar app with a transparent click-through snow overlay, snow that accumulates on the bottom of the screen and on
  window tops, pointer interaction, and the JCB plow and Santa tornado helpers.
